# Vocabulary

Prefer precise graphics terms:

- chamfer, fillet, bevel radius, undercut  
- albedo, roughness, metalness, clearcoat, anisotropy  
- ambient occlusion, cavity, rim light, key/fill/rim  
- silhouette, occluding contour, specular hotspot  
- instancing, LOD, pivot, socket, collider  
- head-unit, landmark, proportion lock  

Avoid: nice, smooth, shiny, cool, better, realistic (alone).
